Focus Blocker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 06, 2025

Overview

Focus Blocker respects your privacy. This extension does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. All settings remain within your browser using the Chrome Storage API (chrome.storage.local / chrome.storage.sync).

What data is stored locally

  • Your custom list of blocked websites
  • Extension enabled/disabled state
  • Snooze timer (time until re-enable)
  • Custom motivational quotes (if you add any)

These values exist only inside your Chrome profile. They are never sent to any external server, developer, or third party.

What we do not collect

  • No browsing history
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking
  • No account information or identifiers
  • No remote logging or telemetry

Data sharing

Focus Blocker does not share, sell, or upload any user data.

Permissions explanation

  • storage — store local settings and quotes
  • declarativeNetRequest — block navigations to user-chosen domains
  • alarms — resume blocking after snooze expires
  • host_permissions — allow redirects only for user-listed domains

Contact

If you have privacy questions or requests, contact: focusblocker@proton.me (replace with your real contact email).
